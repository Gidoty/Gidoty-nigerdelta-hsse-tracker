# Figure data sources

Both figures are generated by `scripts/emas/make_figures.py` directly
from this project's existing outputs — no value is computed fresh for
Fig4, and Fig3's error bars are an exact algebraic consequence of an
already-documented figure, not a new estimate (see below). Re-run the
script to regenerate both files identically.

## Fig3 — national methane slip by year, by destruction efficiency

**Source:** `results/emas/national_totals_by_year.csv`, filtered to
`methaneFractionId == 'x85'` (0.85) and `referenceTemperatureId == '15C'`.

**Columns used:** `year`, `destructionEfficiencyId`,
`total_ch4_slip_tonnes` — one series per `destructionEfficiencyId`
(`design_98`, `plant2022_lit`, `plant2022_effective`; labels and sourced
values from `scripts/emas/scenarios.json`).

**Error bars:** `national_totals_by_year.csv` has no low/high bounds
(those exist only for the central scenario, in
`results/emas/site_year_estimates.csv`). Because the mass-balance formula
is linear in flared volume (`m_CH4_slip = V_g × x_CH4 × ρ_CH4 × (1 − η_f)`),
scaling `V_g` by `(1 ± 0.095)` scales `m_CH4_slip` by the exact same
factor regardless of `η_f`. The error bars are therefore
`total_ch4_slip_tonnes × 0.095` above and below each point — the same
±9.5% volume uncertainty (Elvidge et al. 2016, `scenarios.json`'s
`volumeUncertainty`) already used for the central-case bounds in
`site_year_estimates.csv`, applied algebraically rather than re-run
through the calculator (re-running it would produce the identical number,
since the scaling is exact).

## Fig4 — cumulative share of national flared volume by ranked site

**Source:** `data/public/nigeria_flares.csv` (every row, all three
years), not `results/emas/top20_sites_by_year.csv` (which is truncated
at 20 sites and would not reach 100%).

**Columns used:** `year`, `volume` (BCM — the unit cancels in a cumulative
*share*, so no conversion to m³ is needed here, unlike the methane
calculations).

**Method:** for each year, sort every row's `volume` in descending order,
take the running cumulative sum, and divide by that year's total volume.
Every row is included in the ranking and the total, including the 21
rows with `volume == 0` (documented in
`results/emas/ASSESSMENT_NOTES.md`, anomaly 1) — these rank last and add
nothing to the cumulative sum, matching the convention already used for
volume ranking in `scripts/emas/run_assessment.mjs`'s
`buildTop20SitesByYear` ("adding zero changes nothing").

## Shared notes

- Font: Arial/Helvetica are proprietary and not installable on the Linux
  machine this was built on. Liberation Sans is used instead — it is
  built specifically as an Arial metric-equivalent (identical glyph
  widths), the standard free substitute for this situation. Both figures
  use the same font family at 7–9 pt at final size.
- Format: 600 dpi RGB TIFF (`Fig3.tif`, `Fig4.tif`) plus a vector PDF with
  fully embedded TrueType fonts (`Fig3.pdf`, `Fig4.pdf`), both at exactly
  84 mm × 65 mm.
- Neither figure has a title or caption drawn inside it — captions belong
  in the manuscript/ESM caption list only.
- Series are distinguished by colour, line style (solid/dashed/dotted),
  and marker shape (circle/square/triangle) together, not colour alone.
- All plotted lines are 1.0 pt, above the 0.3 pt minimum.
